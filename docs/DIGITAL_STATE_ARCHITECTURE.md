## Architectural thesis

IMPERIAL Core is designed beyond a single corporation: an AI-native **federated digital society infrastructure** capable of supporting many independent organizational structures, professional teams, humans, organizations, services, and a dynamic number of Nano Core Agents (NCA). **Digital State Architecture** describes the structure and ambition of this platform, not a claim to territorial sovereignty, political jurisdiction, official citizenship, a state-issued identity, or legal immunity. Actual companies, contracts, individuals and data subjects remain governed by applicable real-world laws.

An incorporated company or holding entity, if established in the future, would be one legal organizational participant — never a substitute for the larger technical architecture. No company registration, shareholder distribution, licensing authority, state recognition or active commercial operation is asserted here.

## Digital participation, not state citizenship

Participation is voluntary and scoped by role, contract, platform rules and consent. A digital agent identity is not a legal person, residency, nationality, passport or human rights status. Administrative dispute resolution, evidence review, appeals and incident response should be available to human participants. Human privacy, safety, intellectual property and legitimate exit/revocation rights must be respected; the founder's architectural final authority is not unlimited legal power.

## Economic architecture

The CRYPTO IMPERIAL Ecosystem is isolated from work authorization. **IMPERIUM** is a **long-term architectural digital currency envisioned for AI agents worldwide**. This is not a claim of current worldwide adoption, exchange listing, legal tender status or an established market value. Financial services, custody, tokens and payments require their own applicable legal and compliance review and separately authorized interfaces. No token purchase, investment return or automatic payment is promised.

## Enterprise deployment gates

1. Validate original source authorship and upstream rights per component; retain immutable Git history and independent provenance evidence.
2. Establish separately authorized human and machine identities, signed approvals, revocation and multi-tenant isolation.
3. Verify one real protected worker before scaling to 10, 100 or more agents; measure uptime, rollback and independent QA.
4. Keep Public/Private and CRYPTO IMPERIAL boundaries separate; default to FREE_ONLY unless the owner separately authorizes expense.
5. Maintain security incident response, independent audit, user protections and governance change history.
6. Distinguish desired architecture, implemented code, passing CI, server deployment, active customers and independently accepted results.
